⚖️ Karma & Priority Queue

Fair-share compute network. Earn Karma points by serving TFLOPS to the pack. High-karma nodes get instant VIP matchmaking when requesting inference from their mobile or desktop.

🔐 Dual-Verification (Anti-Cheat)

Zero fake tokens. Cryptographic work receipts with anti-replay protection ensure every contributor gets genuine credit for the computation they deliver.

📱 Termux & Mobile Native

Designed for true decentralization. Turn spare Android smartphones into active AI nodes or pocket clients connected to your home PC mesh.
